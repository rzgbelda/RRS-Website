Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to upload?

A recent quote or invoice from your current supplier, in any common file format. The more line items it has, the more of our catalog we can match it against.

Is the match automatic?

Your file is attached to your quote request, and our team reviews it and matches it to our catalog when preparing your quote — it is not an instant, unreviewed price.

Is there a cost or commitment to try it?

No. Submitting a quote request with a competitor file attached is free and does not commit you to ordering.
