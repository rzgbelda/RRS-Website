How reorder works
Good to know

Before you order.

Still deciding? Call (252) 616-0088 or email sales@roomreadysupply.com.

What's the minimum order?

Most Economy items sell by the case, for example 10 dozen washcloths or 2 dozen sheets. Every card shows exactly what one case holds and what it costs.

How does volume pricing work?

Each product shows its price ladder. Order past a step and the lower price applies to that whole line in your cart, automatically. No code or quote needed.

Can I mix sizes and products?

Yes. Each size is its own product with its own case minimum, and you can combine any of them in one order.

What's the difference between Economy and Premium?

Economy uses T180 and T200 cotton-blend sheets and economy cotton towels to keep cost per room low. Premium steps up to T250 and T300 sheets and ring-spun or long-staple cotton towels for a softer, heavier feel.

How is delivery priced?

Orders ship direct from supplier warehouses. Delivery is calculated for every order and shown in your total before you pay.
