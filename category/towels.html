Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between economy, premium, and luxury towels?

Economy is 100% cotton terry built for high-turnover properties. Premium is an 86/14 cotton-poly blend with a noticeably softer hand. Luxury is 100% ringspun cotton with cam-border construction, for properties where guests expect a spa-level feel.

What sizes do you carry?

Wash cloths, hand towels, bath towels, and bath mats, from 12x12 wash cloths up to 27x54 bath sheets, depending on the tier.

How do I know which tier is right for my property?

Tell us your average daily rate and how often you replace linen, and we will recommend the grade that holds up without overspending.
