Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between bar mops and bar towels?

Our bar mops are 100% cotton, 17 x 20 in., rated by weight per dozen (24 oz. or 30 oz.; heavier holds more), for wiping spills behind the bar and in the kitchen. Bar towels are sold by the dozen.

Do you carry restaurant napkins?

Yes, spun polyester restaurant napkins in 20 x 20 in. solid colors and an 18 x 24 in. bistro stripe, plus white spun polyester bib aprons.

How are foodservice linens sold?

Most items are sold in packs of 12. Each product page shows the pack size and the price steps for larger orders.
