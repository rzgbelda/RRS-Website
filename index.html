Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most from hotels, rentals, and facility teams getting started.

Every product is sold by the case, not individually. Volume pricing kicks in automatically at 6 cases and again at 30 cases — the more you order, the lower your per-case price, with no separate account or negotiation needed.

Orders ship from our warehouse in Plymouth, North Carolina and are typically processed within 3–5 business days after payment is confirmed. Processing time is separate from transit time — see our Shipping Policy for full carrier and delivery details.

No account is required to check pricing or place an order — you can request business pricing or a formal quote directly from the site. Creating a free account does unlock reorder scheduling and order history.

We currently ship within the United States. Some restrictions may apply to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, and locations that can't be serviced by our carriers — see the Shipping Policy for details.
