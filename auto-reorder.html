Auto Reorder Program: Save 5% and Never Run Short

Save 5% on every order and never run short again. Set your schedule once, and we have your supplies ready right on time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my card charged automatically?

Not automatically. On your schedule we prepare your usual order and email you a secure payment link. Your order ships as soon as you complete it, so you stay in control of every delivery.

How much does the Auto Reorder Program save?

You save 5% off your whole order when you set up a reorder schedule, on top of our volume pricing.

What schedules are available?

Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, every 45 days, every 60 days, or a custom interval you set.

Can I cancel or change my schedule?

Yes, anytime, with no contracts. Update your items, frequency or next order date, or cancel, right from your account page.
