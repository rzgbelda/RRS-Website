Auto Reorder Program

Set your supply schedule once. We send your order for approval every time — nothing ships or is charged without your sign-off.

Running out of paper towels or trash liners mid-week usually means someone remembered late. The Auto Reorder Program puts your most-used items on a repeating schedule so restocking isn't something anyone has to keep in their head.

This isn't a subscription that bills you automatically in the background. On your chosen schedule, we send you an order to review and approve — you stay in control of what ships and when.

How it works

1

Pick a schedule

Choose how often you want supplies, from weekly to every 60 days, or set a custom interval.

2

We send the order for approval

On schedule, we prepare your usual order and send it to you to review before anything happens.

3

You approve, we ship

Once you approve, the order goes out. Skip a cycle or change what's in it anytime.

4

Cancel anytime

No contracts. Cancel an active schedule directly from your account page whenever you want.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does auto reorder charge my card automatically?

No. On your schedule, we send you an order to review and approve before anything ships or is charged. Nothing goes out without your sign-off.

What schedules are available?

Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, every 45 days, every 60 days, or a custom interval you set.

Can I cancel or change my schedule?

Yes, anytime, with no contracts. You can cancel an active reorder schedule directly from your account page.
