How reorder works
Good to know

Before you order.

Do I have to buy a full case?

Many items come in both a single piece and a full case. Choose the pack on each product. Case packs carry the lower per-piece price, and items sold only by the case say so next to the price.

How does volume pricing work?

Each product shows its price ladder. Order past a step and the lower price applies to the whole line automatically in your cart. No quote needed.

How is delivery priced?

Orders ship direct from supplier warehouses. Delivery is calculated for every order and shown in your total before you pay, at any order weight.

What's the difference between T200, T300 and T600?

Thread count and fabric. T200 and T300 are durable 60/40 cotton-poly percale. T600 Luxury Blend adds lyocell for a softer, wrinkle-resistant hand, and T600 Wrinkle-Free is 100% Pima cotton sateen that needs no ironing. See the comparison above.
